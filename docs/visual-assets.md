# Фотографии и графика

Файл assets/img/pizza-atlas.png — один фотографический атлас: 3 столбца × 2 ряда, 1536 × 1024 пикселя. В нём Маргарита, Пепперони, Четыре сыра, Мясная, Грибная и Барбекю. CSS выбирает нужную область через background-position; браузер загружает один файл для всего меню. Атлас создан встроенным инструментом imagegen для этого проекта. Это сгенерированные изображения, а не фотографии реального ресторана.

SVG-иконки, знак PizzaPoint и схема доставки созданы кодом. Внешние изображения, шрифты и картографические сервисы при работе приложения не запрашиваются.

Промпт генерации:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one photographic pizza texture atlas for a pizza-delivery app menu. Create ONE landscape image with exact 3:2 aspect ratio, a strict invisible 3-column by 2-row grid of six equal SQUARE cells, ideally 1536 by 1024 pixels. Each cell is a perfectly straight top-down food photograph of ONE complete round freshly baked Italian pizza centered precisely within that square. All six pizzas have the same apparent diameter, occupy about 85% of each square cell, and are completely visible with a small clean margin. Every cell has the SAME flat seamless warm cream background #f6f1e8 and soft subtle natural shadow; NO visible grid lines or frames. Top-left: margherita, melted mozzarella, tomato sauce, scattered fresh basil. Top-middle: pepperoni, generous crisp red pepperoni slices and golden mozzarella. Top-right: four cheeses, golden mozzarella, cheddar, parmesan and tiny blue cheese pieces. Bottom-left: meat pizza with pepperoni, bacon, ham and small roasted chicken pieces. Bottom-middle: mushroom pizza, plentiful sliced brown mushrooms, creamy mozzarella and tiny herb flecks. Bottom-right: barbecue chicken pizza, roast chicken, bacon, purple red onion, fine dark BBQ sauce drizzle. Medium-thin golden puffy crust, appetizing irregular real textures, premium commercial food photography, soft studio light from upper left, rich but natural colors. Maintain identical camera angle and framing between cells. No people, no utensils, no plates, no ingredients outside the pizzas, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. This is a single precisely arranged atlas asset: each cell must contain its own whole pizza.
